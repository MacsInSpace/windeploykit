Binaries the deploy client injects into WinPE at boot (served from http/deploy/,
never written into a WIM). Fetched by scripts/fetch-fieldiso-tools.ps1 into
sidecar/pxe/fieldiso/tools/ and shipped in the app bundle.

7z.exe, 7za.dll, 7zxa.dll
  7-Zip (the 7za standalone build), https://www.7-zip.org/
  Licence: GNU LGPL v2.1 (+ unRAR restriction). Source and licence text at
  https://www.7-zip.org/license.txt and https://www.7-zip.org/download.html.
  Used unmodified to expand vendor driver packs (.exe/.zip/.7z) in WinPE.

curl.exe
  curl for Windows (win64-mingw static build), https://curl.se/windows/
  Licence: the curl licence (MIT/X derivative), https://curl.se/docs/copyright.html
  Used unmodified to POST deployment log lines to the Netboot panel.

Everything else the client runs (dism, diskpart, bcdboot, drvload, expand, net,
reg, find, ipconfig) is part of the user's own Windows boot.wim.
